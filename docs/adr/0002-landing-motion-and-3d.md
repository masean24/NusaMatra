# ADR-0002 — Landing motion, 3D and liquid interactions

- Date: 2026-10-20
- Status: Accepted (implemented in the landing slice)

## Context

Figma 43:713 specifies G1–G11 scroll motion and five 3D zones, and says all 3D should be one Three.js scene with a still image on phones. The owner asked for a premium liquid-spreading navbar interaction, separate from the Normal ↔ Kaca liquid theme transition (Figma 30:1467), without unnecessary animation dependencies.

## Performance and fallbacks

- three.js is a separate lazy chunk (`three-*.js`, ~246 kB gzip). It loads only when a zone is near the viewport, after the browser is idle, and never on viewports narrower than 768px.
- Canvases render on demand (`frameloop="demand"`), cap the pixel ratio at 1.75 and unmount when far off screen, which releases the WebGL context. Geometry and materials are disposed explicitly (`useDisposable`).
- Every zone shows the approved Figma illustration first, and keeps it on phones, without WebGL, or when a scene throws (`SceneErrorBoundary`).

## Deviation from Figma

Figma asks for one shared Three.js scene. The geographic dioramas now come from the external engine; the two host-side zones (3D-2, 3D-3) use one canvas each, mounted only while near the viewport. A shared canvas can be revisited together with the engine team.
